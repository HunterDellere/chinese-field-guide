    <div class="section-head">
      <span class="sh-cn">字源</span>
      <span class="sh-py">zìyuán</span>
      <span class="sh-en">Etymology &amp; Structure</span>
      <span class="sh-rule"></span>
    </div>

    <div class="scholar" data-glyph="实">
      <div class="scholar-label">字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight</div>
      <p><strong>实 shí is an associative compound: the roof 宀 (mián) holds the meaning of a dwelling, and the lower element fills that dwelling with goods.</strong> The picture lives most clearly in the traditional form 實, where the roof 宀 sits over 貫 (guàn), a string of cowrie shells, the old image of wealth strung together. A house packed with strung cowries is a house full of substance: hence solid, substantial, real. The simplified form 实 keeps the same roof but replaces the wealth below with 头, a written shorthand that no longer paints the cowries yet preserves the sense of a full dwelling. The simplified 实 and traditional 實 are one character, the everyday shape on the mainland and the classical shape in Taiwan and Hong Kong.</p>
      <p>From a house full of goods, four senses fan out:</p>
      <p><strong>1. Real, true.</strong> What is actually there, not empty or feigned: 真实 zhēnshí (real), 事实 shìshí (fact), 实话 shíhuà (the truth), 实在 shízài (honestly; really).<br>
      <strong>2. Solid, substantial.</strong> Full rather than hollow, sturdy rather than flimsy: 结实 jiēshi (sturdy), 充实 chōngshí (substantial; enriched), 实心 shíxīn (solid-cored).<br>
      <strong>3. Actual practice.</strong> The real world where things get done, as against mere theory: 实际 shíjì (actual), 实践 shíjiàn (practice), 实现 shíxiàn (to realize), 实验 shíyàn (experiment).<br>
      <strong>4. Fruit, seed.</strong> The full, ripened thing a plant yields: 果实 guǒshí (fruit), and the verb sense of a plant bearing fruit.</p>
      <p>The thread through every sense is fullness as opposed to emptiness. 实 is the standing opposite of 虚 xū (empty, void) and 空 kōng (hollow): wherever 虚 names what is hollow, feigned, or merely theoretical, 实 names what is full, genuine, and put into practice.</p>
    </div>

    <!-- ── ACTUAL ── -->
    <span class="section-anchor" id="actual"></span>
    <div class="section-head">
      <span class="sh-cn">实际</span>
      <span class="sh-py">shíjì</span>
      <span class="sh-en">Actual &amp; Practical: from Theory to Practice</span>
      <span class="sh-rule"></span>
    </div>

    <div class="scholar" data-glyph="实">
      <div class="scholar-label">语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight</div>
      <p>A whole family of high-frequency words pairs 实 with the idea of the real world where action happens. 实际 shíjì is the actual state of affairs as opposed to the abstract or the hoped-for. 实践 shíjiàn is practice, the doing that tests an idea against reality. 实现 shíxiàn is to make real, to turn a plan or a dream into an accomplished fact: 实现梦想 "to realize a dream." 实验 shíyàn is the experiment, the controlled test that lets reality answer a question. Across all of them, 实 carries the same charge: the empty or the merely imagined is brought down to the solid ground of what actually is.</p>
      <p>The contrast that organizes this family is 理论 vs. 实践, theory versus practice, and 实际 vs. 事实, the actual versus the factual. The note below sorts those two near-twins.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="cards">

      <!-- 实际 -->
      <div class="card c-ochre">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">实际</span>
          <span class="card-py">shíjì</span>
          <span class="card-en">actual; real; practical; reality</span>
        </div>
        <div class="tags"><span class="tag tag-adj">ADJ 形容词 xíngróngcí</span></div>
        <div class="card-def">
          实 shí (real) + 际 jì (juncture; situation). The actual state of things as opposed to the theoretical: 实际情况 "the actual situation," 实际上 "actually, in fact." Of a person, 很实际 means practical and realistic.
        </div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">实际情况比我们想的复杂。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Shíjì qíngkuàng bǐ wǒmen xiǎng de fùzá.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">The actual situation is more complicated than we thought.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
        <div class="note">
          <span class="note-label">辨析 biànxī · 实际 vs. 事实</span>
          <strong>事实</strong> shìshí is a single concrete fact, a discrete thing that is the case ("it is a fact that..."). <strong>实际</strong> shíjì is the whole actual situation taken together, often set against theory or expectation. You report 一个事实 (one fact); you act according to 实际 (the real circumstances). 实际上 means "as a matter of fact"; you cannot swap in 事实上 everywhere, though they overlap.
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="card c-ochre">
        <div class="card-head">
