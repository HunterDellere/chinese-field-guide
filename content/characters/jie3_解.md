    <!-- ── ETYMOLOGY ── -->
    <span class="section-anchor" id="etymology"></span>
    <div class="section-head">
      <span class="sh-cn">字源</span>
      <span class="sh-py">zìyuán</span>
      <span class="sh-en">Etymology &amp; Structure: Cutting Apart the Ox</span>
      <span class="sh-rule"></span>
    </div>

    <div class="scholar" data-glyph="解">
      <div class="scholar-label">字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight</div>
      <p>解 is an associative compound, and its picture is unusually vivid. The graph gathers three elements: 角 jiǎo (the horn of an ox), 刀 dāo (a knife), and 牛 niú (an ox). The scene is a butchering: a hand takes a knife to an ox and pries the horn away from the head, separating the parts of the carcass cleanly along their natural joins. The <strong>radical 角</strong>, the horn, is the part the eye fixes on, the thing being detached. From that one act of <em>cutting cleanly apart</em> the whole tree of meaning grows.</p>
      <p>The Shuōwén Jiězì 说文解字 glosses 解 as 判也, to divide, to cut apart, and notes the ox horn and the knife in the graph. Hold that literal sense first: to take a bound, joined, knotted, or whole thing and separate it into its parts. Every later meaning is a metaphor on that one motion. <em>Untie</em> a rope: separate what was tied. <em>Solve</em> a problem: separate the tangle into pieces that come free. <em>Explain</em> a text: take a dense passage apart so its sense lies open. <em>Dissolve</em> a substance: break it apart into a solution. In each case 解 undoes a bind.</p>
      <p>解 is a polyphone with three readings. The primary reading <strong>jiě</strong> covers the great family above: to untie, to solve, to explain, to dissolve, to relieve. A second reading <strong>jiè</strong> means to escort or send a prisoner under guard (押解 yājiè, 解送 jièsòng), the sense of <em>conveying</em> something away. A third reading <strong>xiè</strong> survives mainly as a surname and in 解数 xièshù (a set of skills or moves, as of a martial artist). When you meet 解, default to jiě; reach for jiè only in the narrow legal-escort sense and xiè only for the name or the skill word.</p>
    </div>

    <!-- ── PATTERNS / BIANXI ── -->
    <span class="section-anchor" id="patterns"></span>
    <div class="section-head">
      <span class="sh-cn">辨析</span>
      <span class="sh-py">biànxī</span>
      <span class="sh-en">To Understand vs To Be Acquainted With: 理解 vs 了解</span>
      <span class="sh-rule"></span>
    </div>

    <div class="pattern">
      <span class="label">辨析 biànxī · Two kinds of knowing</span>
      <strong>理解 lǐjiě</strong> (理 = principle, reason): to <em>understand</em> in the sense of grasping the logic, the reasoning, the why of something. You 理解 an idea, an argument, a person's feelings. It is comprehension from the inside, getting the sense to come clear.<br>
      <strong>了解 liǎojiě</strong> (了 = to finish, to know fully): to <em>be acquainted with</em>, to know the facts about, to have information on. You 了解 a situation, a city, a candidate's background. It is knowledge from gathered facts, knowing the lay of a thing.<br>
      A clean test: 理解 is grasping <em>meaning</em>; 了解 is possessing <em>information</em>. You can 了解 every fact about a person yet still not 理解 why they acted as they did.
    </div>

    <div class="pattern">
      <span class="label">速记 sùjì · Quick test</span>
      <strong>Did the sense come clear in your mind?</strong> → 理解 lǐjiě. (我理解你的意思 = I understand what you mean; 互相理解 = mutual understanding.)<br>
      <strong>Do you know the facts and background?</strong> → 了解 liǎojiě. (我很了解他 = I know him well; 了解情况 = look into the situation.)
    </div>

    <!-- ── SOLVE & EXPLAIN ── -->
    <span class="section-anchor" id="solve"></span>
    <div class="section-head">
      <span class="sh-cn">解决</span>
      <span class="sh-py">jiějué</span>
      <span class="sh-en">To Solve &amp; To Explain</span>
      <span class="sh-rule"></span>
    </div>

    <div class="scholar" data-glyph="解">
      <div class="scholar-label">语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight</div>
      <p>Two of the most useful 解-words sit in the realm of thought. To <strong>solve</strong> a problem (解决 jiějué, 解答 jiědá, 解题 jiětí) is to take a tangle and separate it until the answer comes free, exactly the ox-and-knife motion turned inward. To <strong>explain</strong> a thing (解释 jiěshì, 讲解 jiǎngjiě) is to take it apart in words so the listener can see its inner structure. The shared image is unwinding: a problem is a knot you loosen, an explanation is a dense thing you open up. The opposite, 误解 wùjiě, is to take a thing apart <em>wrongly</em> and so misread its sense.</p>
    </div>

    <!-- ── DISSOLVE & RELIEVE ── -->
    <span class="section-anchor" id="dissolve"></span>
    <div class="section-head">
      <span class="sh-cn">溶解</span>
      <span class="sh-py">róngjiě</span>
      <span class="sh-en">To Dissolve &amp; To Relieve</span>
      <span class="sh-rule"></span>
    </div>

    <div class="scholar" data-glyph="解">
      <div class="scholar-label">语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight</div>
      <p>The most physical branch of 解 brings the knife back to matter. To <strong>dissolve</strong> (溶解 róngjiě) is to break a solid apart until it spreads through a liquid, the ox-and-knife separation taken down to the molecular scale. From there 解 reaches the body's needs: to <strong>relieve</strong> or <strong>satisfy</strong> a pressing want, as in 解渴 jiěkě (to quench thirst) and 解饿 jiě'è (to stave off hunger). The logic holds: thirst is a bind on the body, and to 解 it is to undo that pressure. Even 解手 jiěshǒu, the polite phrase for relieving oneself, runs on the same image of release.</p>
    </div>

      <div class="scholar-label">记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image</div>
      <p>See the graph for what it draws: a knife 刀 prying an ox horn 角 from the ox 牛, separating a whole thing cleanly into its parts. Hold that one motion and every sense of 解 falls out. Untie what is bound (解开, 解放, 解除, 解雇). Loosen the knot in thought, the problem that comes free and the meaning made plain (解决, 解释, 理解, 误解, 解答). Break a thing apart in matter, and relieve a pressure on the body (溶解, 解渴). Each is the same blade undoing the same kind of bind.</p>
      <p>Two things to lock in. First the reading: 解 is jiě almost everywhere, jiè only in the narrow escort-under-guard sense (押解), and xiè only as the surname or the skill word 解数. Second the pair: 理解 lǐjiě is grasping the <strong>reason</strong>, while 了解 liǎojiě is knowing the <strong>facts</strong>. You can 了解 every detail about someone and still fail to 理解 why they did it.</p>
