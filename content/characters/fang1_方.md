    <div class="section-head">
      <span class="sh-cn">字源</span>

    <div class="scholar" data-glyph="方">
      <div class="scholar-label">字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight</div>
      <p><strong>方 fāng is an ancient pictograph whose exact original referent is debated.</strong> The oracle-bone form shows a shape often read as a plough with a crossbar, or alternatively as two boats lashed side by side; the <em>Shuōwén Jiězì</em> glosses it as boats joined together. What matters for the modern character is the idea that settled onto the glyph: a flat, four-sided, oriented thing. 方 became the word for "square," and from squareness came the whole family of meaning.</p>
      <p>Four senses branch from that core:</p>
      <p><strong>1. Square, the shape.</strong> 正方形 zhèngfāngxíng (square), 长方形 chángfāngxíng (rectangle), 方块 fāngkuài (a block, a cube).<br>
      <strong>2. Direction and place.</strong> A square has four sides, so 方 names orientation: 方向 fāngxiàng (direction), 东方 dōngfāng (the east), 地方 dìfang (place).<br>
      <strong>3. Method and way.</strong> The path by which something is approached: 方法 fāngfǎ (method), 方式 fāngshì (way, manner), and the prescription a doctor writes, 方子 fāngzi.<br>
      <strong>4. Side or party.</strong> One of two parties to a matter: 对方 duìfāng (the other party), 双方 shuāngfāng (both sides).</p>
      <p>The thread is orientation. A square has fixed sides and corners, and from that fixity 方 supplies the language of where a thing is, which way it faces, and how it is to be done. As an adverb it also means "just now," the moment squarely at hand.</p>

        <div class="card-def">

          </div>
        </div>
        <div class="note">
          <span class="note-label">辨析 biànxī · 方法 vs. 方式</span>
          <strong>方法</strong> is the concrete procedure or technique, the steps you follow. <strong>方式</strong> fāngshì is the broader manner or style of doing something: 生活方式 "way of life," 思维方式 "way of thinking." A 方法 solves a task; a 方式 characterizes a whole approach.
        </div>
      </div>

    <div class="scholar" data-glyph="方" style="margin-top:2.5rem;">
      <div class="scholar-label">记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image</div>
      <p>Hold a square in mind. It has four equal sides and four fixed corners, and everything 方 means radiates from that fixity. The four sides become the four directions (东方, 西方, 南方, 北方). A fixed spot on the ground becomes a place (地方). The settled procedure for a task becomes a method (方法). And one of the two facing sides of a matter becomes a party to it (对方).</p>
      <p>When 方 appears as an adverb meaning "just now," picture the present moment as the corner squarely under your foot: the point you are standing on right now. From the geometry of the square, the whole vocabulary of orientation and approach falls out.</p>
