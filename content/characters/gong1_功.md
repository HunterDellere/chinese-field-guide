    <div class="scholar" data-glyph="功">
      <div class="scholar-label">字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight</div>
      <p><strong>功 gōng</strong> is a pictophonetic compound: the radical <strong>力 lì</strong> (strength; the picture of a straining muscle or a plough) carries the meaning, and <strong>工 gōng</strong> (work, craft) carries the sound. The semantic logic is transparent: strength applied to work produces a result worth honoring. Where 工 names the labor itself, 功 names what that labor <em>achieves</em>: the merit, the accomplishment, the credit that accrues.</p>
      <p>This is the character's central insight. 功 is not raw effort and not bare success, but the bridge between them: effort that has crystallized into something measurable. A soldier who wins a battle has 功 (merit); a student who drills characters for years builds 功 (skill); a medicine that cures has 功 (efficacy). The same glyph spans the battlefield, the practice hall, and the laboratory because Chinese treats all three as the same act: strength patiently converted into result.</p>
      <p>The 力 radical gathers a family of effort-and-result characters around it: 努 nǔ (to strive), 加 jiā (to add), 助 zhù (to assist), 劳 láo (toil), 励 lì (to encourage), 勤 qín (diligent). In modern physics, 功 was chosen to translate the technical term <em>work</em>: 做功 zuò gōng is to do work in the mechanical sense, force acting through distance, a perfect reuse of a two-thousand-year-old word.</p>

    <span class="section-anchor" id="merit"></span>
    <div class="section-head">
      <span class="sh-cn">功劳</span>
      <span class="sh-py">gōngláo</span>
      <span class="sh-en">Merit &amp; Success</span>
      <span class="sh-rule"></span>
    </div>

    <div class="cards">

      <div class="card c-red">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">功劳</span>
          <span class="card-py">gōngláo</span>
          <span class="card-en">merit: credit for meritorious service</span>
        </div>
        <div class="tags"><span class="tag tag-n">N 名词 míngcí</span></div>
        <div class="card-def">
          功 gōng (merit) + 劳 láo (toil; labor). The credit owed to someone for hard work that produced real results. Used to acknowledge contribution: 这是大家的功劳 = this is everyone's accomplishment. The modest counterpart is 苦劳 kǔláo, credit merely for effort even without success. The set phrase 没有功劳也有苦劳 = "even if there's no merit, there's at least the hard work," a gentle plea not to overlook someone's toil.
        </div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">这次项目成功，他的功劳最大。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Zhè cì xiàngmù chénggōng, tā de gōngláo zuì dà.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">In the success of this project, his contribution was the greatest.</div>
          </div>
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">别把所有功劳都归于自己。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Bié bǎ suǒyǒu gōngláo dōu guīyú zìjǐ.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Don't claim all the credit for yourself.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="card c-red">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">成功</span>
          <span class="card-py">chénggōng</span>
          <span class="card-en">success: to succeed</span>
        </div>
        <div class="tags"><span class="tag tag-vn">V/N 动名词 dòng-míngcí</span></div>
        <div class="card-def">
          成 chéng (to accomplish; to become) + 功 gōng (achievement). Literally "to complete the achievement." Both verb (to succeed) and noun (success). One of the most common words in modern Chinese, the natural opposite of 失败 shībai (failure). 成功人士 = a successful person. 取得成功 = to attain success.
        </div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">经过多年努力，他终于成功了。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Jīngguò duō nián nǔlì, tā zhōngyú chénggōng le.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">After many years of effort, he finally succeeded.</div>
          </div>
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">这次实验取得了成功。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Zhè cì shíyàn qǔdé le chénggōng.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">This experiment was a success. <em>[noun use]</em></div>
          </div>
        </div>
        <div class="note">
          <span class="note-label">辨析 biànxī · 成功 vs. 成就</span>
          <strong>成功</strong> chénggōng = succeeding, the event or state of having achieved a goal (a process reaching its end). <strong>成就</strong> chéngjiù = an accomplishment, the concrete achievement itself, often a body of lasting work: 一生的成就 = a lifetime's achievements. You 成功 at one thing; your 成就 are what you have built across a career.
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="card c-red">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">立功</span>
          <span class="card-py">lì gōng</span>
          <span class="card-en">to render meritorious service: to perform a deed</span>
        </div>
        <div class="tags"><span class="tag tag-v">V 动词 dòngcí</span></div>
        <div class="card-def">
          立 lì (to establish; to set up) + 功 gōng (merit). To establish merit through a notable deed, originally military, now also civic and professional. A verb-object compound, so it splits: 立了大功 = rendered great service. Often paired with reward: 立功受奖 = to perform a deed and receive a commendation.
        </div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">他在抗洪救灾中立了大功。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Tā zài kàng hóng jiùzāi zhōng lì le dà gōng.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">He rendered great service in the flood-relief effort.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
        <div class="note">
          <span class="note-label">相关 xiāngguān · Related</span>
          <strong>功绩</strong> gōngjì = achievements and contributions (a record of deeds). <strong>功臣</strong> gōngchén = a meritorious official, a minister who earned the ruler's trust through service. <strong>论功行赏</strong> lùn gōng xíng shǎng = to grant rewards according to merit.
        </div>
      </div>

    </div><!-- /cards merit -->

    <span class="section-anchor" id="effort"></span>
    <div class="section-head">
      <span class="sh-cn">功夫</span>
      <span class="sh-py">gōngfu</span>
      <span class="sh-en">Effort &amp; Skill</span>
      <span class="sh-rule"></span>
    </div>

    <div class="cards">

      <div class="card c-teal">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">功夫</span>
          <span class="card-py">gōngfu</span>
          <span class="card-en">skill from practice; effort; kung fu</span>
        </div>
        <div class="tags"><span class="tag tag-n">N 名词 míngcí</span></div>
        <div class="card-def">
          功 gōng (effort; achievement) + 夫 fu (a worker; here a light suffix). The skill that only accumulated time and practice can produce. Three linked senses: the abstract effort invested (花了很多功夫 = spent a lot of effort), the time it takes (一会儿功夫 = in a moment), and the trained skill itself, which in the martial-arts sense entered English as <em>kung fu</em>. The famous philosophy: 功夫不负有心人 = effort never lets down the dedicated person.
        </div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">他的书法很有功夫。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Tā de shūfǎ hěn yǒu gōngfu.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">His calligraphy shows real skill.</div>
          </div>
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">学好中文要下很大的功夫。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Xuéhǎo Zhōngwén yào xià hěn dà de gōngfu.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Learning Chinese well takes a great deal of effort.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
        <div class="note">
          <span class="note-label">文化 wénhuà · Culture</span>
          <strong>下功夫</strong> xià gōngfu = to put in the work, to apply sustained effort to mastering something. The mindset behind 功夫 is that excellence is not talent but patient, repeated practice, the same idea that gives 功夫茶 gōngfu chá (the slow, careful gongfu tea ceremony) its name.
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="card c-teal">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">用功</span>
          <span class="card-py">yònggōng</span>
          <span class="card-en">to study hard; diligent</span>
        </div>
        <div class="tags"><span class="tag tag-v">V 动词 dòngcí</span></div>
        <div class="card-def">
          用 yòng (to use; to apply) + 功 gōng (effort). To apply one's effort, specifically to study. Used as a verb (用功读书 = to study diligently) and as an adjective describing a hard-working student (他很用功 = he studies very hard). Distinct from 努力 nǔlì, which applies to effort in any domain; 用功 leans toward study and learning.
        </div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">她从小就很用功，成绩一直很好。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Tā cóngxiǎo jiù hěn yònggōng, chéngjì yīzhí hěn hǎo.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">She has studied hard since childhood, and her grades have always been good.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="card c-teal">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">基本功</span>
          <span class="card-py">jīběngōng</span>
          <span class="card-en">basic skills; fundamentals</span>
        </div>
        <div class="tags"><span class="tag tag-n">N 名词 míngcí</span></div>
        <div class="card-def">
          基本 jīběn (basic; fundamental) + 功 gōng (skill). The foundational skills of any discipline, the drills a beginner must master before anything else: scales for a musician, stances for a martial artist, strokes for a calligrapher. To 练基本功 (drill the fundamentals) is treated as the non-negotiable bedrock of mastery.
        </div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">想学好武术，必须先练好基本功。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Xiǎng xuéhǎo wǔshù, bìxū xiān liànhǎo jīběngōng.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">To master martial arts, you must first drill the fundamentals.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>

    </div><!-- /cards effort -->

      <span class="sh-en">Function &amp; Efficacy</span>
      <span class="sh-rule"></span>

    <div class="cards">

      <div class="card c-violet">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">功能</span>
          <span class="card-py">gōngnéng</span>
          <span class="card-en">function: capability of a thing</span>
        </div>
        <div class="tags"><span class="tag tag-n">N 名词 míngcí</span></div>
        <div class="card-def">
          功 gōng (achievement; effect) + 能 néng (ability; capacity). The function or capability of a device, organ, or system, what it is able to do. Ubiquitous in technology and biology: 手机功能 = phone features, 多功能 = multifunctional, 功能强大 = powerful (rich in capability), 身体机能 / 器官功能 = bodily and organ function.
        </div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">这款手机的功能非常强大。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Zhè kuǎn shǒujī de gōngnéng fēicháng qiángdà.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">This phone's features are extremely powerful.</div>
          </div>
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">肝脏有解毒的功能。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Gānzàng yǒu jiědú de gōngnéng.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">The liver has the function of detoxification.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="card c-violet">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">功效</span>
          <span class="card-py">gōngxiào</span>
          <span class="card-en">efficacy: the effect produced</span>
        </div>
        <div class="tags"><span class="tag tag-n">N 名词 míngcí</span></div>
        <div class="card-def">
          功 gōng (effect) + 效 xiào (effect; result). The efficacy of a medicine, treatment, or method, the actual beneficial result it achieves. Especially common in medicine and traditional Chinese health: 这种药的功效很好 = this medicine is very effective. Close to 效果 xiàoguǒ (effect; result), but 功效 stresses the beneficial, intended power of something.
        </div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">这种草药有清热解毒的功效。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Zhè zhǒng cǎoyào yǒu qīngrè jiědú de gōngxiào.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">This herb has the efficacy of clearing heat and removing toxins.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="card c-violet">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">武功</span>
          <span class="card-py">wǔgōng</span>
          <span class="card-en">martial skill: fighting prowess</span>
        </div>
        <div class="tags"><span class="tag tag-n">N 名词 míngcí</span></div>
        <div class="card-def">
          武 wǔ (martial; military) + 功 gōng (skill). Skill in the martial arts, the trained fighting ability central to wuxia fiction and film. 武功高强 = of formidable martial skill. 一身武功 = a whole body of fighting skill. The word also has a classical sense of military accomplishment, the deeds that win wars.
        </div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">小说里的大侠武功高强。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Xiǎoshuō lǐ de dàxiá wǔgōng gāoqiáng.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">The hero in the novel possesses formidable martial skill.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
        <div class="note">
          <span class="note-label">物理 wùlǐ · In Physics</span>
          A separate technical sense: <strong>做功</strong> zuò gōng = to do work in the mechanical sense (force acting through distance). 功率 gōnglǜ = power (rate of doing work). Here 功 is the standard physics translation of <em>work</em>.
        </div>
      </div>

      <p>See 功 as a <strong>worker's arm (力) lifting at a workbench (工).</strong> Strength meets craft, and out of the meeting comes a result worth honoring. That single image holds every sense at once: the soldier whose strength wins a deed earns 功劳 (merit); the student whose strength is poured into years of practice builds 功夫 (skill); the machine or medicine whose strength accomplishes something measurable has 功能 and 功效 (function and efficacy).</p>
      <p>The lesson the character itself teaches is patience. 功亏一篑 warns that strength withdrawn one basket short of the summit leaves nothing but a pile of earth, while 功夫不负有心人 promises that strength steadily applied always, in the end, pays back the one who applied it.</p>
