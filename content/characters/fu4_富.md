    <span class="section-anchor" id="etymology"></span>
    <div class="section-head"><span class="sh-cn">字源</span><span class="sh-py">zìyuán</span><span class="sh-en">Etymology &amp; Structure</span><span class="sh-rule"></span></div>
    <div class="scholar" data-glyph="富">
      <div class="scholar-label">字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight</div>
      <p>富 = 宀 + 畐. The roof 宀 (mián) sits on top as the semantic element, the same sheltering cover that roofs 家 (jiā, home) and 安 (ān, peace). Beneath it stands 畐 (fú), a full-bellied wine jar swollen at the middle, which gives both the sound and a clear hint at the meaning: fullness. A brimming vessel kept safe indoors is the oldest image of wealth, grain and wine stored against the lean season.</p>
      <p>The Shuōwén Jiězì glosses 富 as 備也, "to be complete, fully provisioned," and adds 厚也, "thick, ample." The seal-script form keeps the roof arched over the bulging jar, the wide waist of 畐 drawn to show a container packed to capacity. Wealth here means stockpile, a household whose shelves are loaded.</p>
      <p>From that concrete granary the sense widened. A full storehouse means a rich family (富有), and the same fullness applied to anything that comes in great quantity gives 丰富, abundant or rich in variety. The character can also turn active, 富国 meaning to enrich the state, the verb folding back into the original picture of filling a vessel.</p>
      <p>Japanese keeps the glyph as 富 (fu, tomi), seen in 富士山 Fujisan and the word 富裕 (fuyū) for affluence, the same roof-over-jar shape carrying the same idea of plenty across the sea.</p>

    <span class="section-anchor" id="wealth"></span>
    <div class="section-head"><span class="sh-cn">财富</span><span class="sh-py">cáifù</span><span class="sh-en">Having Wealth</span><span class="sh-rule"></span></div>
    <div class="cards">
      <div class="card c-red">
        <div class="card-head"><span class="card-cn">富有</span><span class="card-py">fùyǒu</span><span class="card-en">wealthy; rich in</span></div>
        <div class="tags"><span class="tag tag-adj">Adj 形容词 xíngróngcí</span></div>
        <div class="card-def">富 (rich) plus 有 (to have). Literally "rich and possessing." Describes a person with great wealth, and figuratively something abundantly endowed with a quality, as in 富有经验 (rich in experience).</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example"><div class="ex-cn">他出生在一个富有的家庭。</div><div class="ex-py">Tā chūshēng zài yí gè fùyǒu de jiātíng.</div><div class="ex-en">He was born into a wealthy family.</div></div>
          <div class="example"><div class="ex-cn">这位老师富有耐心。</div><div class="ex-py">Zhè wèi lǎoshī fùyǒu nàixīn.</div><div class="ex-en">This teacher is full of patience.</div></div>
        </div>
        <div class="note"><span class="note-label">辨析 biànxī · 富有 vs. 有钱</span>有钱 (yǒuqián) is plain and conversational, "has money." 富有 is more formal and can extend to abstract riches like experience or imagination.</div>
      </div>

        <div class="card-def">财 (money, property) plus 富 (riches). The accumulated stock of wealth itself, the noun for what a rich person holds. Often paired abstractly, as in 精神财富 (spiritual wealth).</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example"><div class="ex-cn">健康是最宝贵的财富。</div><div class="ex-py">Jiànkāng shì zuì bǎoguì de cáifù.</div><div class="ex-en">Health is the most precious wealth.</div></div>

        </div>

    <span class="section-anchor" id="abundance"></span>
    <div class="section-head"><span class="sh-cn">丰富</span><span class="sh-py">fēngfù</span><span class="sh-en">Abundance</span><span class="sh-rule"></span></div>
    <div class="scholar" data-glyph="富">
      <div class="scholar-label">用法 yòngfǎ · Usage Note</div>
      <p>When 富 leaves the bank account it describes plenty of any kind. Paired with 丰 (fēng, lush, ample), it becomes 丰富, the everyday word for a rich variety of things: a varied menu, a full life, a deep store of knowledge. The same jar that once held wine now holds whatever the sentence stocks it with.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="cards">
      <div class="card c-violet">

        <div class="tags"><span class="tag tag-vn">Adj/V 形/动</span></div>
        <div class="card-def">丰 (lush, plentiful) plus 富 (abundant). As an adjective, rich in variety or quantity. As a verb, to enrich or broaden, as in 丰富知识 (to broaden one's knowledge).</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example"><div class="ex-cn">这家餐厅的菜很丰富。</div><div class="ex-py">Zhè jiā cāntīng de cài hěn fēngfù.</div><div class="ex-en">This restaurant has a rich variety of dishes.</div></div>
          <div class="example"><div class="ex-cn">旅行能丰富我们的人生经验。</div><div class="ex-py">Lǚxíng néng fēngfù wǒmen de rénshēng jīngyàn.</div><div class="ex-en">Travel can enrich our life experience.</div></div>
        </div>
        <div class="note"><span class="note-label">辨析 biànxī · 丰富 vs. 富有</span>丰富 stresses variety and quantity (a rich array). 富有 leans toward possessing wealth or being deeply endowed with a single quality.</div>

    <div class="scholar" data-glyph="富" style="margin-top:2.5rem;">
      <div class="scholar-label">记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image</div>
      <p>Picture a low country house at dusk. Push the wooden door and the air is sweet and heavy: under the broad sloping roof 宀 stands a single fat-bellied wine jar 畐, glazed and round at the waist, filled to the lip and stoppered with cloth. Beside it the shelves bow under sacks of grain. The roof keeps the rain off, the jar keeps the plenty in. That sheltered, brimming jar is 富, wealth as a full vessel kept safe.</p>
      <p>The top stroke is always the roof, the same 宀 that covers 家 and 安. Whatever the character spends its meaning on, the storehouse is the source. Pour money into the jar and you get 财富 and 富有. Pour variety in and the jar overflows with 丰富 dishes and 丰富 experience. Let a whole village fill its jars and you get 富裕; let a nation fill its granaries and arm its soldiers and you get 富强, rich and strong.</p>
