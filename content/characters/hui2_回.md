    <!-- ── ETYMOLOGY ── -->
    <span class="section-anchor" id="etymology"></span>
    <div class="section-head">
      <span class="sh-cn">字源</span>
      <span class="sh-py">zìyuán</span>
      <span class="sh-en">Etymology &amp; Structure</span>
      <span class="sh-rule"></span>
    </div>

    <div class="scholar" data-glyph="回">
      <div class="scholar-label">字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight</div>
      <p><strong>回 huí is a pictograph of water coiling back on itself.</strong> The oracle-bone form is a spiral, the swirl of an eddy or whirlpool turning in on its own center. Over time the curves squared off into the modern shape: a small enclosure 口 nested inside a larger one 囗, one loop within another. The original picture of circling water gave the character its core meaning, to turn back, to come around again.</p>
      <p>From the coiling motion, the senses unwind:</p>
      <p><strong>1. To return, to go back.</strong> The basic movement homeward: 回家 huí jiā (to go home), 回来 huílái (to come back), 回去 huíqù (to go back).<br>
      <strong>2. To turn around in word or mind.</strong> Sending something back the way it came: 回答 huídá (to answer), 回信 huíxìn (to reply by letter), 回忆 huíyì (to recall, to turn the mind back).<br>
      <strong>3. A time, an occurrence (measure word).</strong> One turn of an event: 一回 yì huí (once), 这回 zhè huí (this time), 上回 shàng huí (last time).<br>
      <strong>4. A chapter of a novel.</strong> Classical novels count their installments in 回, each a turn of the story: 《红楼梦》一百二十回 "Dream of the Red Chamber in 120 chapters."</p>
      <p>The thread is the turn back. Whether the body returns home, a letter returns an answer, the mind returns to a memory, or a story turns to its next chapter, 回 is the motion of coming back around, the eddy completing its loop.</p>

        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">回来</span>
          <span class="card-py">huílái</span>
          <span class="card-en">to come back; to return (toward the speaker)</span>
        </div>
        <div class="tags"><span class="tag tag-v">V 动词 dòngcí</span></div>
        <div class="card-def">
          回 huí (return) + 来 lái (come, toward here). To come back to where the speaker is. Its mirror is 回去 huíqù, to go back to somewhere away from the speaker. The direction of 来 / 去 sets the point of view.
        </div>

          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">他昨天晚上才回来。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Tā zuótiān wǎnshang cái huílái.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">He only got back last night.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
        <div class="note">
          <span class="note-label">辨析 biànxī · 回来 vs. 回去</span>
          <strong>回来</strong> is returning toward where the speaker stands: 快回来 "come back here." <strong>回去</strong> huíqù is returning away from the speaker, back to where one came from: 我该回去了 "I should head back (home)." The choice tracks the speaker's location, exactly like 来 and 去.
        </div>

    <span class="section-anchor" id="measure"></span>
    <div class="section-head">
      <span class="sh-cn">一回</span>
      <span class="sh-py">yì huí</span>
      <span class="sh-en">A Time — the Measure Word</span>
      <span class="sh-rule"></span>
    </div>

    <div class="scholar" data-glyph="回">
      <div class="scholar-label">语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight</div>
      <p>Because a return is one completed turn, 回 became a measure word for occurrences, much like 次 cì. You can say 来过一回 or 来过一次 ("came once") with little difference, though 回 is warmer and more colloquial, at home in spoken northern Mandarin. The time-words built on it are everyday: 这回 zhè huí (this time), 上回 shàng huí (last time), 下回 xià huí (next time).</p>
      <p>One idiom turns on the measure word itself: 那是另一回事 nà shì lìng yì huí shì, "that is another matter entirely," literally "another one-turn affair." And the classical storyteller's hook 且听下回分解 qiě tīng xià huí fēnjiě, "and now hear the next chapter unfold," uses 回 in its chapter sense, the tale turning to its next loop.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="scholar" data-glyph="回" style="margin-top:2.5rem;">
      <div class="scholar-label">记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image</div>
      <p>See an eddy in a stream, water coiling back on its own center, one loop inside another. That spiral, squared into two nested boxes, is the whole character. Every meaning of 回 is the eddy completing its turn.</p>
      <p>The body loops back home (回家, 回来). A question loops back as an answer (回答). The mind loops back into the past (回忆). The head turns and a wanderer comes back from a wrong road (回头是岸). And each completed turn of an event is counted as 一回, one time around. Wherever something comes back to where it began, the eddy of 回 is turning.</p>
