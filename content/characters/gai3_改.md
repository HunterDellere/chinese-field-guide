    <div class="section-head"><span class="sh-cn">字源</span><span class="sh-py">zìyuán</span><span class="sh-en">Etymology &amp; Structure</span><span class="sh-rule"></span></div>
    <div class="scholar" data-glyph="改">
      <div class="scholar-label">字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight</div>
      <p>改 = 己 + 攵. On the left, 己 (jǐ) sits as a small kneeling figure; on the right, 攵 is the old picture of a hand gripping a stick, the same striking element that drives 教 (to teach) and 政 (to govern). The character is a scene, not a sound-spelling: a raised rod comes down over a crouching child, and the child is made to be different than it was. The 攵 side is semantic, the action of forcing; the 己 side is semantic too, the small body that the action lands on.</p>
      <p>The seal form keeps both parts clear, the bent kneeling shape on the left and the hand-and-rod on the right. From this disciplinary image the Shuōwén Jiězì reads 改 as 更也, to make over, to renew. The path from concrete to abstract is short here. A blow that reshapes a child becomes any deliberate act that reshapes a thing: a draft, a habit, a law, a route.</p>
      <p>That origin still tilts the modern word. 改 is rarely passive drift. It carries an agent and an intention, often the sense of fixing what was wrong rather than merely swapping one state for another. To 改 a sentence is to correct it; to 改 a habit is to break and remake it; to 改 a name is to choose a new one on purpose. The stick is gone from daily speech, but the force of correction it implied is still inside the glyph.</p>
      <p>Japanese carries 改 as かい / あらた-める (kai / aratameru), as in 改正 (kaisei, revision) and 改札 (kaisatsu, the ticket gate where a stub is altered). Korean reads it 개 (gae), in 개혁 (gaehyeok, reform) and 개정 (gaejeong, amendment). Across all three the word leans the same way, toward correcting and reforming rather than idle change.</p>

    <span class="section-anchor" id="change"></span>
    <div class="section-head"><span class="sh-cn">变易</span><span class="sh-py">biànyì</span><span class="sh-en">To Change, To Alter</span><span class="sh-rule"></span></div>
    <div class="cards">
      <div class="card c-red">
        <div class="card-head"><span class="card-cn">改变</span><span class="card-py">gǎibiàn</span><span class="card-en">to change, to transform</span></div>
        <div class="tags"><span class="tag tag-vn">V/N 动名 dòngmíng</span></div>
        <div class="card-def">改 (alter) + 变 (change). The broadest everyday word for change: to make something different, or the difference itself. Works on situations, minds, fates, and plans.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example"><div class="ex-cn">这件事改变了我的一生。</div><div class="ex-py">Zhè jiàn shì gǎibiàn le wǒ de yìshēng.</div><div class="ex-en">This event changed the whole course of my life.</div></div>
          <div class="example"><div class="ex-cn">他不想改变主意。</div><div class="ex-py">Tā bù xiǎng gǎibiàn zhǔyì.</div><div class="ex-en">He doesn't want to change his mind.</div></div>
        </div>
        <div class="note"><span class="note-label">辨析 biànxī · 改变 vs. 变化</span>改变 needs an agent who acts on something; 变化 (biànhuà) is change that simply happens, observed from outside.</div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-ochre">
        <div class="card-head"><span class="card-cn">改善</span><span class="card-py">gǎishàn</span><span class="card-en">to improve, to better</span></div>
        <div class="tags"><span class="tag tag-vn">V/N 动名 dòngmíng</span></div>
        <div class="card-def">改 (alter) + 善 (good). To change toward the good: to better conditions, relations, or quality. Used for situations and systems more than for a person's skill.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example"><div class="ex-cn">政府努力改善居民的生活。</div><div class="ex-py">Zhèngfǔ nǔlì gǎishàn jūmín de shēnghuó.</div><div class="ex-en">The government is working hard to improve residents' lives.</div></div>
        </div>
        <div class="note"><span class="note-label">辨析 biànxī · 改善 vs. 改进</span>改善 betters a condition (relations, environment, living standards); 改进 (gǎijìn) betters a method or product through refinement.</div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-teal">
        <div class="card-head"><span class="card-cn">改天</span><span class="card-py">gǎitiān</span><span class="card-en">another day, some other time</span></div>
        <div class="tags"><span class="tag tag-adj">Adv 副词 fùcí</span></div>
        <div class="card-def">改 (change) + 天 (day). To shift a plan to a different day. A soft, friendly way to postpone or to propose meeting later, very common in spoken Mandarin.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example"><div class="ex-cn">今天太忙了，咱们改天再聊吧。</div><div class="ex-py">Jīntiān tài máng le, zánmen gǎitiān zài liáo ba.</div><div class="ex-en">I'm too busy today; let's talk another time.</div></div>
        </div>

    <div class="cards">

        <div class="tags"><span class="tag tag-v">V 动词 dòngcí</span></div>
        <div class="card-def">修 (mend, trim) + 改 (alter). To go back over a text, plan, or law and revise it. The standard word for editing a document or amending a draft.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example"><div class="ex-cn">老师让我修改这篇作文。</div><div class="ex-py">Lǎoshī ràng wǒ xiūgǎi zhè piān zuòwén.</div><div class="ex-en">The teacher asked me to revise this essay.</div></div>
          <div class="example"><div class="ex-cn">这份合同还需要修改几个地方。</div><div class="ex-py">Zhè fèn hétóng hái xūyào xiūgǎi jǐ ge dìfang.</div><div class="ex-en">This contract still needs a few places revised.</div></div>
        </div>
        <div class="note"><span class="note-label">辨析 biànxī · 修改 vs. 改正</span>修改 reworks and polishes a whole text; 改正 targets a specific mistake and sets it right.</div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-sienna">

        <div class="card-def">改 (alter) + 正 (correct, upright). To change a wrong thing into a right one: a mistake, an error, a fault. The 正 spells out the destination, correctness itself.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example"><div class="ex-cn">请改正你作业里的错误。</div><div class="ex-py">Qǐng gǎizhèng nǐ zuòyè lǐ de cuòwù.</div><div class="ex-en">Please correct the mistakes in your homework.</div></div>
        </div>
      </div>

        <div class="tags"><span class="tag tag-v">V 动词 dòngcí</span></div>
        <div class="card-def">改 (alter) + 成 (become). A result complement: to alter A so that it becomes B. Names the new state explicitly after the verb.</div>

        <div class="note"><span class="note-label">辨析 biànxī · 改革 vs. 改良</span>改革 overhauls the structure; 改良 (gǎiliáng) improves within it, refining a crop strain or a method without tearing it down.</div>

    <div class="scholar" data-glyph="改" style="margin-top:2.5rem;">
      <div class="scholar-label">记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image</div>
      <p>Picture a small child, 己, kneeling on a packed-earth floor, head bowed over a writing slate. Behind the child stands a teacher whose right hand grips a thin bamboo rod, 攵, raised and ready. The rod taps once on the slate, the child rubs out the crooked stroke and draws it straight. That single corrective tap is 改: not the child changing on a whim, but a hand reaching in to set the line right.</p>
      <p>Hold the two halves apart and the glyph reads itself. The 己 on the left is the thing being worked on, the draft, the habit, the mistake. The 攵 on the right is the hand that comes down to fix it. Wherever 改 appears, look for that hand and that intention. 改正 sets a wrong stroke straight, 修改 goes over the whole essay rod in hand, 改革 brings the rod down on an entire institution and remakes it like hide tanned into leather.</p>
      <p>Keep the rod always pointed at a flaw. That is why 改天 feels gentle but 改革 feels heavy: both are the same correcting hand, one resetting a coffee date, the other resetting a country.</p>
