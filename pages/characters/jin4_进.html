字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

The traditional form 進 = 辶 (chuò, the walk radical) + 隹 (zhuī, a short-tailed bird). The 辶 element on the left is the ancient sign for movement along a road, a foot on a path. The 隹 inside it is a bird, and the old reading turns on a fact of nature: a bird can only ever walk forward, never backward. So the picture is movement that goes ahead and never reverses, which is exactly the sense of the word: to advance, to go forward, to enter.

The simplified form 进 swaps the bird 隹 for 井 jǐng (a well), a tidier shape that is easier to write. The walk radical 辶 stays put, so the modern character still reads as movement on the road, even though the inner element no longer carries the bird image.

Three senses branch from the foot on the forward path:

1. To enter, to go in. Movement across a threshold into a space: 进来 jìnlái (come in), 进去 jìnqù (go in), 进入 jìnrù (to enter).
2. To advance, to make progress. Movement ahead in space or in skill: 前进 qiánjìn (to advance), 进步 jìnbù (progress), 进展 jìnzhǎn (to make headway).
3. To take in, to carry out. Movement of goods or action inward: 进口 jìnkǒu (to import), 进行 jìnxíng (to carry out), 进食 jìnshí (to take food).

The thread through every sense is forward and inward. Whether a person crosses a doorway, a student climbs in skill, or a country brings goods across its border, 进 is the bird that only walks ahead, the foot that moves in and never back. Its standing opposite is 退 tuì, to retreat.

构词 gòucí Word-Formation Patterns
构词规律 gòucí guīlǜ · Forward and Inward 进 as "to enter" → 进来 come in · 进去 go in · 进入 to enter · 走进 to walk in
进 as "to advance / progress" → 前进 to advance · 进步 progress · 进展 to make headway · 上进 to better oneself
进 as "to carry out / take in" → 进行 to carry out · 进口 to import · 引进 to introduce · 进食 to take food
进 paired with its opposite 退 → 进退 advance-and-retreat · 进出 in-and-out
进来 jìnlái To Enter: the Come/Go Pair
进来 jìnlái to come in (toward the speaker)
V 动词 dòngcí
进 jìn (to enter) + 来 lái (to come). To enter moving toward where the speaker is. 请进来 "please come in" is what you say from inside a room to someone at the door.
请进来坐。
Qǐng jìnlái zuò.
Please come in and sit down.
外面冷，快进来吧。
Wàimiàn lěng, kuài jìnlái ba.
It's cold outside, hurry and come in.
进去 jìnqù to go in (away from the speaker)
V 动词 dòngcí
进 jìn (to enter) + 去 qù (to go). To enter moving away from where the speaker stands. From outside a building you tell someone 你先进去 "you go in first."
门开了，我们进去吧。
Mén kāi le, wǒmen jìnqù ba.
The door is open, let's go in.
辨析 biànxī · 进来 vs. 进去 The difference is the speaker's vantage point. 进来 means entering toward the speaker, so you use it when you are already inside. 进去 means entering away from the speaker, so you use it when you are still outside. The same come/go split runs through 出来 / 出去, 回来 / 回去, and 上来 / 上去.
进入 jìnrù to enter; to go into (a place or stage)
V 动词 dòngcí
进 jìn (to enter) + 入 rù (to go in). A more formal "to enter," used of physical spaces and of abstract stages alike: 进入房间 "enter the room," 进入新阶段 "enter a new phase."
飞机进入了云层。
Fēijī jìnrù le yúncéng.
The plane entered the clouds.
进步 jìnbù Advance & Progress
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

The forward sense of 进 is where the character earns its everyday weight. Step forward in space and you have 前进 qiánjìn, to advance; step forward in skill or condition and you have 进步 jìnbù, progress. The same root powers 上进 shàngjìn, the drive to better oneself, and 进展 jìnzhǎn, the headway a project makes over time. In each the picture is the same forward-walking bird, now climbing rather than merely crossing a threshold.

Its constant foil is 退 tuì, to retreat. The two are so tightly bound that they fuse into single words: 进退 jìntuì (advance and retreat, i.e. one's room to maneuver) and the idiom 进退两难, caught with no good move in either direction. To know 进 well is to feel 退 standing right behind it.

进步 jìnbù progress; to improve; progressive
V/N 动名词 dòngmíngcí
进 jìn (to advance) + 步 bù (a step). To move forward step by step, hence to improve. Works as a verb (你进步了 "you've improved") and as a noun (很大的进步 "great progress").
你的中文进步很快。
Nǐ de Zhōngwén jìnbù hěn kuài.
Your Chinese is improving fast.
前进 qiánjìn to advance; to go forward; to march ahead
V 动词 dòngcí
前 qián (front; forward) + 进 jìn (to advance). To move ahead in space or in cause: 向前进 "advance forward," 勇往直前 the spirit of pressing on. Its opposite is 后退 hòutuì, to fall back.
队伍继续向前进。
Duìwu jìxù xiàng qián jìn.
The procession kept advancing forward.
进展 jìnzhǎn to make progress; headway; development
V/N 动名词 dòngmíngcí
进 jìn (to advance) + 展 zhǎn (to unfold). The headway a task, plan, or situation makes over time: 工作有了进展 "the work has made progress." More about an undertaking than a person.
谈判进展顺利。
Tánpàn jìnzhǎn shùnlì.
The negotiations are progressing smoothly.
辨析 biànxī · 进步 vs. 进展 进步 is about a person or thing getting better, measured against its old self: you 进步 in a language or a skill. 进展 is about a task or situation moving along its course: a project, a case, or a negotiation 进展. One praises improvement, the other reports headway.
进行 jìnxíng Proceed & Take In
进行 jìnxíng to carry out; to conduct; to be underway
V 动词 dòngcí
进 jìn (to advance) + 行 xíng (to go; to act). A formal verb meaning to carry an activity forward: 进行讨论 "hold a discussion," 进行研究 "conduct research." It pairs with a two-syllable action noun and lends a deliberate, official tone.
会议正在进行。
Huìyì zhèngzài jìnxíng.
The meeting is in progress.
进口 jìnkǒu to import; imports; entrance (of a place)
V/N 动名词 dòngmíngcí
进 jìn (to take in) + 口 kǒu (mouth; opening). To bring goods in across a border, the opposite of 出口 chūkǒu (to export). The same word also names a physical "entrance," the opening you go in through.
这些水果是进口的。
Zhèxiē shuǐguǒ shì jìnkǒu de.
These fruits are imported.
引进 yǐnjìn to introduce; to bring in (technology, talent, methods)
V 动词 dòngcí
引 yǐn (to draw; to lead) + 进 jìn (to take in). To bring something in from outside, typically from abroad: 引进技术 "introduce technology," 引进人才 "bring in talent."
公司引进了新设备。
Gōngsī yǐnjìn le xīn shèbèi.
The company brought in new equipment.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
突飞猛进 tū fēi měng jìn to advance by leaps and bounds: to progress at great speed Lit: sudden-fly-fierce-advance. Said of rapid, dramatic progress in study, technology, or an economy. 科技突飞猛进 "technology advances by leaps and bounds."
循序渐进 xún xù jiàn jìn to advance step by step in proper order: to progress gradually Lit: follow-sequence-gradually-advance. The opposite of rushing: it praises learning or building that proceeds in the right order, one stage at a time. A favorite phrase about study method.
与时俱进 yǔ shí jù jìn to advance together with the times: to keep pace with change Lit: with-the-times-together-advance. To move forward in step with the age rather than lag behind it. A common phrase praising people or institutions that stay current.
进退两难 jìn tuì liǎng nán hard to advance and hard to retreat: caught in a dilemma Lit: advance-retreat-both-hard. To be stuck where neither going forward nor falling back will do, the classic no-win bind. The 进 / 退 pairing makes the trap vivid.
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

Picture a short-tailed bird on a road, the 隹 inside the walk radical 辶 of the traditional 進. A bird hops only forward, never back, so the whole character is forward motion that cannot reverse. Hold that single image and every sense follows.

Walk that bird across a threshold and you have entering (进来, 进去, 进入). Walk it ahead in skill and you have progress (进步, 前进). Walk goods in across a border and you have importing (进口, 引进). And set 退, retreat, behind it, and you feel the tension that powers 进退两难: the bird that can only go forward, caught at a spot where forward is blocked too.
