字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

间 jiān = 门 (mén, gate) enclosing 日 (rì, sun). The picture is light showing through the crack between a pair of closed doors: the gap itself, the space that lies between. The traditional form 間 sets 月 (yuè, moon) in the gateway instead, moonlight slipping through the door at night; the simplified 间 swaps in 日, the sun. Either way the meaning is the same, the chink of light that marks an opening, an interval, a space-between.

From that gap, four senses open out:

1. Space between, interval. The literal gap: 之间 zhījiān (between), 空间 kōngjiān (space), 间隔 jiàngé (an interval).
2. A span of time. The interval read on the clock and calendar: 时间 shíjiān (time), 期间 qījiān (a period), 中间 (in the middle of a stretch).
3. A room. A bounded space within a building, and the measure word for counting such spaces: 房间 fángjiān (room), 一间屋子 yì jiān wūzi (one room).
4. Among, within a domain. The space inside a sphere of life: 人间 rénjiān (the human world), 民间 mínjiān (among the people, folk), 田间 (in the fields).

A second reading, jiàn, foregrounds the gap as separation: 间接 jiànjiē (indirect, by way of an intermediary) and 离间 líjiàn (to sow discord, to drive a wedge between). The thread through every sense is the same opening between the doors: a gap in space, in time, or in the fabric of a relationship.

时间 shíjiān Time & Interval
时间 shíjiān time; a period of time; the time (on the clock)
N 名词 míngcí
时 shí (hour; time) + 间 jiān (interval). Time as a measurable span, the everyday word for it: 没有时间 "no time," 花时间 "to spend time," 什么时间 "at what time." Time understood as the interval between two moments.
我今天没有时间。
Wǒ jīntiān méiyǒu shíjiān.
I don't have time today.
学中文需要很多时间。
Xué Zhōngwén xūyào hěn duō shíjiān.
Learning Chinese takes a lot of time.
辨析 biànxī · 时间 vs. 时候 时间 is time as a quantity or span you can have, spend, and run out of: 有时间 "to have time." 时候 shíhou is a point or occasion, the "when" of something: 小时候 "when (one was) little," 什么时候 "when?" One measures duration; the other locates a moment.
期间 qījiān period; during the time of
N 名词 míngcí
期 qī (a fixed period) + 间 jiān (interval). A defined stretch of time, often used after an event to mean "during": 假期期间 "during the holidays," 会议期间 "in the course of the meeting."
假期期间我去了北京。
Jiàqī qījiān wǒ qù le Běijīng.
During the holidays I went to Beijing.
瞬间 shùnjiān an instant; the blink of an eye
N 名词 míngcí
瞬 shùn (a blink) + 间 jiān (interval). The smallest interval of time, the moment it takes to blink: 一瞬间 "in a flash." The interval shrunk almost to nothing.
那一瞬间，我什么都明白了。
Nà yí shùnjiān, wǒ shénme dōu míngbai le.
In that instant, I understood everything.
中间 zhōngjiān Between & Among
中间 zhōngjiān middle; center; in between; among
N 名词 míngcí
中 zhōng (center) + 间 jiān (space). The space in the middle, whether of a row, a room, or a stretch of time: 坐在中间 "to sit in the middle," 在两座山中间 "between two mountains."
他坐在我们中间。
Tā zuò zài wǒmen zhōngjiān.
He sits in the middle of us.
之间 zhījiān between; among (more formal)
PHR 短语 duǎnyǔ
之 zhī (classical possessive) + 间 jiān (space between). The bookish, frequent way to say "between": 朋友之间 "between friends," 国与国之间 "between nations." It frames the space within a relationship or a set.
朋友之间应该互相信任。
Péngyǒu zhījiān yīnggāi hùxiāng xìnrèn.
Friends should trust one another.
空间 kōngjiān space; room (abstract); scope
N 名词 míngcí
空 kōng (empty) + 间 jiān (space). Empty space, both physical and figurative: 没有空间 "no room," 发展空间 "room to grow." Paired with 时间, the two name space and time together.
这个房间空间很大。
Zhège fángjiān kōngjiān hěn dà.
This room has a lot of space.
房间 fángjiān Room, the Measure Word
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

A room is a bounded space within walls, so the "space-between" character naturally came to name it. 房间 fángjiān is the everyday word for a room, and 间 also serves as the measure word for counting them: 一间房 yì jiān fáng (one room), 三间教室 sān jiān jiàoshì (three classrooms), 一间小店 (a small shop). When you count rooms, shops, or other walled spaces, 间 is the word that does the counting.

The same logic builds the names of specific rooms: 车间 chējiān (a workshop, the "vehicle space" of a factory floor), 卫生间 wèishēngjiān (bathroom, the "hygiene room"), 洗手间 xǐshǒujiān (washroom). In each, 间 marks off one enclosed space from the rest of the building, the architectural cousin of the gap between the doors.

房间 fángjiān room
N 名词 míngcí
房 fáng (house; room) + 间 jiān (a bounded space). The standard word for a room in a house or hotel: 我的房间 "my room," 订一个房间 "to book a room."
我的房间在三楼。
Wǒ de fángjiān zài sān lóu.
My room is on the third floor.
卫生间 wèishēngjiān bathroom; toilet; restroom
N 名词 míngcí
卫生 wèishēng (hygiene) + 间 jiān (room). The polite, standard word for a bathroom in homes and public buildings. A common synonym is 洗手间 xǐshǒujiān.
请问卫生间在哪里？
Qǐngwèn wèishēngjiān zài nǎlǐ?
Excuse me, where is the bathroom?
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
字里行间 zì lǐ háng jiān within the words and between the lines: the meaning an author implies but does not state Lit: inside-the-characters, between-the-lines. The unspoken sense that lives in the spaces of a text. 字里行间流露出真情 "true feeling shows between the lines."
间不容发 jiān bù róng fà the gap admits not a hair: a situation of extreme danger or urgency Lit: the-gap-does-not-allow-a-hair. The interval is so narrow not even a single hair could fit, so the margin for error is none at all. Here 间 takes the jiān reading for the gap itself.
亲密无间 qīn mì wú jiàn close with no gap between: utterly intimate, inseparable Lit: intimate-close-without-gap. Of a friendship or bond so tight that no space, no estrangement, divides the two. Here 间 takes the jiàn reading, the gap as separation.
挑拨离间 tiǎo bō lí jiàn to stir up trouble and drive a wedge: to set people against each other Lit: stir-up and sow-division. To deliberately create a gap between people who were close, by rumor or provocation. 离间 (jiàn) is to open a rift where there was none.
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

Picture a pair of closed doors, 门, with a thin blade of sunlight, 日, showing through the crack where they meet. That sliver of light is the whole character: 间 is the gap between, the space-between made visible.

Widen the gap and it becomes a span of time (时间), the interval between two moments. Set it in a row of people and it becomes the middle (中间). Wall it off and it becomes a room you can count one by one (一间房). Open it inside a relationship and it becomes either closeness with no gap (亲密无间) or the wedge that drives people apart (离间). Wherever 间 goes, look for the space between two things.
