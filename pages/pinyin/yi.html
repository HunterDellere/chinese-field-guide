yi

Every character on Jiǎoluò Shūwū read as yi in any tone — 4 readings total across the four tones. Pick the tone you mean.

一 yī 1st · high level 1 reading
二 yí 2nd · rising no entries yet
三 yǐ 3rd · low / dipping 1 reading 四 yì 4th · falling 2 readings

If you have the sound yi but not the character, here are the 4 characters read this way: 一 (yī, one; unified; as soon as), 以 (yǐ, by means of; using; because; in order to), 意 (yì, meaning, intention, the wish of the mind), 义 (yì, righteousness, duty, justice). They split across 3 tones, so the tone is what separates them.
