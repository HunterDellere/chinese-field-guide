//角落書屋/Slices/Tea
茶	chá	tea — The character that gave the world its word for tea: a grass over wood over man, a plant that shaped civilization, and the locus of China's richest material culture.
茶道	chá dào	the way of tea — From wild forest leaf to civilizational ritual: how tea became China's quintessential art of presence.
茶具	chá ju	Tea Implements — The vessels, tools, and objects of the tea table: from the Yixing purple clay teapot to the fairness pitcher, each implement with a name, a function, and an aesthetic logic.
工夫茶	gōng fu chá	Gongfu Tea — Tea prepared with skill and time: the Southern Chinese brewing method that defines serious tea culture, built on small vessels, multiple short steeps, and total attention.
白茶	bái chá	white tea — minimally processed; wither and dry
白毫	bái háo	white down — the fine hairs on young buds
茶馆	chá guǎn	Teahouse — Teahouse: a social institution especially important in Sichuan and Guangdong, where one goes not just to drink tea but to play mahjong, listen to storytelling, or simply spend a morning reading the paper.
茶毫	chá háo	tea down — fine white hairs on young leaves
茶荷	chá hé	tea display dish — for showing the dry leaf
茶壶	chá hú	Teapot — Teapot. The Yixing purple clay teapot (紫砂壶) is the prestige form, prized because it absorbs tea oils over time and improves with use.
茶黄素	chá huáng sù	theaflavins — the brightness compounds of black tea
茶夹	chá jiā	tea tongs — for handling hot cups
茶马古道	Chá Mǎ Gǔ Dào	Ancient Tea-Horse Road — the trade route that carried tea to Tibet
茶盘	chá pán	tea tray — the base of the gongfu setup
茶叶	chá yè	Tea leaves , the dry product before brewing — Tea leaves , the dry product before brewing. 茶 alone can refer to the beverage, the plant, or the leaves.
茶叶罐	chá yè guàn	tea canister / storage jar
茶艺	chá yì	tea art; the art and ceremony of tea — 茶 chá + 艺 yì (art; skill, the same character as in 艺术 fine art). The performance and aesthetic dimension of tea preparation. 茶艺师 cháyìshī = tea master; a professional practitioner of the ceremony. 茶艺表演 = a tea ceremony performance.
茶针	chá zhēn	tea needle / pu-erh pick
发酵	fā jiào	oxidation (lit. "fermentation") — the enzymatic browning step

公道杯	gōng dào bēi	fairness pitcher / sharing pitcher
枸杞	gǒu qǐ	wolfberry / goji berry — the ubiquitous health supplement
古树茶	gǔ shù chá	old-tree tea — material from trees over 100 years old
黑茶	hēi chá	dark tea & pu-erh — the other tea category prized for aging
红茶	hóng chá	black tea (literally "red tea") — 红 hóng (red) + 茶 chá (tea). Fully oxidized tea, the category English calls black tea. The Chinese name describes the amber-red color of the infusion in the cup; the English name describes the near-black color of the oxidized leaf.
花草茶	huā cǎo chá	tisanes & herbal infusions — not true tea
黄茶	huáng chá	yellow tea — the rarest of the six categories
回甘	huí gān	returning sweetness — the lingering aftertaste
金花	jīn huā	golden flowers — the prized mold colonies in Fu Brick
金芽	jīn yá	golden bud — the premium single-bud grade
叩手礼	kòu shǒu lǐ	knocking hand ritual — tapping thanks
老白茶	lǎo bái chá	aged white tea — white tea stored for 3+ years
凉茶	liáng chá	cooling tea — Guangdong herbal brews for clearing heat
龙井	Lóng jǐng	Longjing — Dragon Well green tea — From 龙坞 Dragon Hollow near Hangzhou, Zhejiang. China's most famous green tea , flat-pressed leaves, clean chestnut aroma, and a 1,200-year history. Imperial tribute tea. 西湖龙井 Xī Hú Lóngjǐng (West Lake Dragon Well) is the protected-origin premium grade.

绿叶红边	lǜ yè hóng biān	green leaf red edge — the visual sign of oolong oxidation
毛茶	máo chá	rough tea / mao cha — the uncompressed base material
闷黄	mèn yuáng	smothering / yellowing — the defining yellow tea step
明前茶	míng qián chá	pre-Qingming tea — the highest harvest grade
泡茶	pào chá	to brew tea; to steep tea — 泡 pào (to soak; to steep; to foam) + 茶 chá (tea). The everyday verb for making tea. 你喝什么茶？我去泡 "What tea would you like? I'll go steep it." Also: 泡茶时间 steeping time · 第一泡 the first infusion.

A post-fermented tea that improves with age like wine; some vintage cakes sell for astronomical sums.
