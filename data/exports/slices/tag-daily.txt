//角落書屋/Slices/Daily life
电	diàn	electricity, electric, telegram — Lightning descending from a rain cloud, a character that jumped from describing the weather to naming the force that defines modern civilization.
时	shí	time, hour, season — The sun moving: time as something visible, measurable, and morally weighted.
的确	dí qu è	indeed; certainly — a frozen adverb containing 的 — 的确 is pronounced dí-què (not de), showing that 的 in fixed compounds retains its old full pronunciation. 这的确是个好主意 "This is indeed a good idea." A reminder that 的 has a fuller historical identity beyond its grammatical particle role.
雷电交加	léi diàn jiāo jiā	thunder and lightning together — a violent storm; used of chaotic, intense situations — Lit: thunder-lightning-together-intensify. Describes a fierce storm where both thunder (雷 léi) and lightning (电 diàn) are striking simultaneously. Used metaphorically for any situation of simultaneous, intense upheaval: a chaotic meeting, an emotional confrontation, a turbulent period.
时不我待	shí bù wǒ dài	time will not wait for me — seize the moment; there is no time to lose — Lit: time-not-me-wait. A classical inversion , the subject (时, time) is placed before the negation, with "me" as object, producing the archaic feel of a proverb. The phrase frames time as an agent with no personal loyalty: it moves whether you are ready or not.
时过境迁	shí guò jìng qiān	time passes, circumstances change — things are different now; that was then — 时过 (time has passed) + 境迁 (the situation has shifted , 境 jìng: circumstances, context; 迁 qiān: to move, to change). Used to acknowledge that what was true, appropriate, or possible in the past no longer applies , without judgment, simply as a statement of temporal fact.
岁月不饶人	suì yu è bù ráo rén	the years spare no one — time is merciless; age catches up with everyone — 岁月 suìyuè (years and months , a classical compound for the flow of time) + 不饶 bù ráo (shows no mercy, does not spare). A cousin-phrase to 时不我待, but the mood is different: less urgent, more resigned. Where 时不我待 urges action, 岁月不饶人 acknowledges what cannot be undone.
一触即发	yī chù jí fā	touch and it ignites — a situation on the verge of explosion — Lit: one-touch-immediately-fire. A crisis situation where the slightest action will trigger an explosion: political tensions, a fragile negotiation, volatile emotions. Originally describing hair-trigger mechanisms, now widely applied to any situation of extreme tension.
比如说	bǐ rú shuō	"For example" or "like, for instance — "For example" or "like, for instance." Introduces a concrete illustration of an abstract point. Among the most useful phrases for explaining anything. The shorter 比如 works identically; 比如说 just adds a beat of preparation.
不过	bù guò	"However" or "but" (softer) — "However" or "but" (softer). The default conversational pivot. Lighter than 但是, which can feel direct or even adversarial in spoken contexts. 不过 carries a gentler "that said…" tone, ideal for adding a qualification.
不会吧	bù huì ba	"No way!" or "you can't be serious — "No way!" or "you can't be serious." Expresses disbelief, often at bad news or an unwelcome outcome. The 吧 softens it from an outright denial into an incredulous reaction.
不一定	bù yī dìng	"Not necessarily — "Not necessarily." A gentle pushback that leaves room for the other person's view to be partly right. Often used to introduce a qualification: 不一定吧, 也可能是… ("not necessarily, it could also be…").
充电	chōng diàn	to charge (device)
电话	diàn huà	telephone; a phone call — 电 diàn (electric) + 话 huà (speech). Speech carried on electricity, the telephone, and by extension the call itself: 打电话 dǎ diànhuà "to make a phone call," 接电话 "to answer the phone."
电脑	diàn nǎo	Computer — Computer: electric brain. 台式电脑 is a desktop, 笔记本 a laptop. The formal 计算机 persists in degree names and government documents.
电影	diàn yǐng	film; movie — lit. "electric shadow" — 电 diàn (electric) + 影 yǐng (shadow; reflection; image). Film, movie , named for the projected shadows of early cinema. 看电影 = to watch a film. 电影院 = cinema. 电影节 = film festival (Cannes 戛纳电影节, Venice 威尼斯电影节, Berlin 柏林电影节, Hong Kong 香港电影节).
电子	diàn zǐ	electronic; electron — 电 diàn (electric) + 子 zǐ (small particle; child). As a physics term: the electron. As a prefix: electronic, digital. 电子产品 = electronics (products). 电子书 = e-book. 电子商务 = e-commerce (also shortened to 电商 diànshāng). 电子政务 = e-government. 电子竞技 = esports (also 电竞 diànjìng).
对了	duì le	Oh right! — used when remembering something — 对 duì (correct) + 了 le (change of state). "Oh right!", used to introduce something you just remembered or to redirect a conversation. Not about correctness in this use: 对了 at the beginning of a sentence is a discourse marker meaning "Oh! I just remembered…" or "Wait, by the way…".
对我来说	duì wǒ lái shuō	"For me" or "as far as I'm concerned — "For me" or "as far as I'm concerned." Frames a statement as true from your perspective without claiming it is universally true. Often paired with adjectives of preference or difficulty: 对我来说, 这个太难了 ("for me, this is too hard").
发电	fā diàn	to generate electricity
反而	fǎn ér	on the contrary; instead; the other way around — 反 fǎn (reverse) + 而 ér (and so). Marks an outcome that runs opposite to expectation: you would predict one thing, but the reverse happens. 不但没…反而… "not only did not... but on the contrary...".
分钟	fēn zhōng	minute (of time) — 分 fēn (a divided unit) + 钟 zhōng (clock). A minute as a span of time: 等五分钟 "wait five minutes." To tell the time on the clock face, 分 alone is enough: 两点十五分 "two fifteen."
改天再聊	gǎi tiān zài liáo	"Let's chat another time — "Let's chat another time." Proposes future contact without committing to a specific time. Warm and noncommittal, like English "let's catch up soon." Common closer among friends and acquaintances.
怪不得	guài bu de	"That explains it!" Synonym for 难怪 with the same function — "That explains it!" Synonym for 难怪 with the same function. Slightly more drawn out and emphatic. 怪不得他不来 ("that's why he didn't come").
关键在于	guān jiàn zài yú	"The key is" or "what matters is — "The key is" or "what matters is." Marks the core of an argument. Useful when you want to highlight the deciding factor among several you have just laid out: 关键在于时间 ("the key is timing").
光	guāng	light, ray
还有一点	hái yǒu yī diǎn	"One more thing" or "and another point — "One more thing" or "and another point." Marks the addition of a further item to a list of points you are making. Common in explanations and arguments where you are building up to a conclusion.
话说回来	huà shuō huí lái	"Coming back to what we were saying" or "on the other hand — "Coming back to what we were saying" or "on the other hand." Marks a return to a previous thread, often with a contrasting take. Useful for acknowledging a counterpoint to your own argument: "but coming back to it, you do have a point that…"
换个话题	huàn ge huà tí	"Let's change the subject — "Let's change the subject." Direct topic-change marker, used when the current thread has run its course or when you want to move on from something uncomfortable. Slightly more deliberate than 对了; signals an intentional shift rather than a stumble onto something new.
换句话说	huàn jù huà shuō	in other words — Literally swap a sentence and say it: 换 (swap) + 句话 (a sentence) + 说 (say). A fixed connective that restates a point another way, exactly like English "to put it differently."
回到刚才的话题	huí dào gāng cái de huà tí	"Coming back to what we were talking about — "Coming back to what we were talking about." Returns to an earlier thread after a digression. Particularly useful in longer conversations where you want to close out a tangent and pick up the original point.
吉利	jí lì	auspicious; lucky; propitious — Auspicious, lucky, propitious , the standard term for something considered to bring good fortune. A number, date, phrase, or object that is 吉利 is sought after and displayed. Its opposite: 不吉利 (bù jílì), inauspicious.
季节	jì jié	season
夹	jiā	To pick up with chopsticks — To pick up with chopsticks: the specific verb for the gripping action of chopsticks. 夹菜 (jiā cài) is to pick up food from a shared dish.
简单地说	jiǎn dān de shuō	"To put it simply" or "in short — "To put it simply" or "in short." Introduces a condensed version of a longer explanation. Useful when you have been speaking at length and want to summarize the core point.
据我所知	jù wǒ suǒ zhī	"As far as I know — "As far as I know." A hedge that acknowledges your information may be incomplete. Useful when stating something you believe to be true but do not want to assert with full confidence. Carries a slightly bookish, considered tone.
口语	kǒu yǔ	spoken language
筷子架	kuài zi jià	Chopstick rest — Chopstick rest: a small ceramic or metal rest placed beside the bowl to lay chopsticks on between bites. Used in formal settings; absent in casual ones.
老实说	lǎo shí shuō	"Frankly" or "to be straight with you — "Frankly" or "to be straight with you." Close cousin to 说实话, with the same function. 老实 literally means "honest, straightforward." Slightly warmer in tone, often used among friends before offering an opinion the listener might not want to hear.
连接词	lián jiē cí	connector word
另外	lìng wài	"Besides" or "in addition — "Besides" or "in addition." Marks a related but separate point. Useful when you want to add information that is not strictly part of the same argument: 另外, 我想问你一件事 ("also, I want to ask you something").
流利	liú lì	fluent
没错	méi cuò	that's right; exactly; no mistake — 没 méi (not have) + 错 cuò (error). "No error here," used to agree firmly: 没错，我就是这个意思 "exactly, that's just what I mean." Often softened to 没错儿 in northern speech.
秒	miǎo	second (time)
那个	nà ge	that one — "Uh…" or "that…" The single most common verbal filler in Mandarin, used to stall for half a second while the next word arrives. Stretched out as 那个… 那个… it functions almost identically to English "um." Overusing it sounds hesitant; sprinkling it in sounds native.
那就这样吧	nà jiù zhè yàng ba	"Okay, let's leave it at that — "Okay, let's leave it at that." The standard wrap-up marker. Signals that the conversation has reached a natural stopping point and you are about to close. The 吧 invites the other party to agree to wind down.
难怪	náng uài	"No wonder!" The realization that a previously confusing situation now makes sense given new information. 难怪你那么累 ("no wonder you're so tired") after learning the other person worked all night. Compact and very natural.
你知道吗	nǐ zhī dào ma	"You know?" Functions like English "you know?" both as a sincere check-in and as discourse filler. Useful for keeping the listener engaged mid-sentence. Overused in casual conversation by younger speakers, where it can become as habitual as English "like."
然而	rán ér	"However" (formal) — "However" (formal). The more literary cousin of 不过, common in written and formal spoken contexts. In casual conversation it can sound bookish; in lectures, presentations, and considered discussions it fits naturally.
让我想想	ràng wǒ xiǎng xiang	"Let me think — "Let me think." Direct, slightly more formal than 那个. Appropriate when a question deserves a real answer and you want the listener to wait while you produce one. The reduplicated 想想 softens it, suggesting brief consideration rather than a long pause.
时机	shí jī	the right moment; timing; an opportune occasion — 时 shí (time) + 机 jī (machine; pivot; crucial moment; opportunity). 时机 is the Confucian classical meaning of 时 made into a compound: the moment that is ripe, when conditions align. It is strategic time, the narrowing window before it closes.
是吗	shì ma	"Is that so?" or "really?" Slightly cooler than 真的吗, often signaling polite interest without strong surprise. The neutral acknowledgment that you have heard and registered what was just said.
是这样啊	shì zhè yàng a	"Oh, so it's like that — "Oh, so it's like that." A softer, gentler version of 原来如此. The 啊 particle gives it a reflective, taking-it-in quality. Useful when someone has just shared something personal or explained a situation.
手机	shǒu jī	mobile phone — lit. "hand machine" — 手 shǒu (hand) + 机 jī (machine). China's most common word for mobile phone , the machine held in the hand. Completely replaced the earlier 手提电话 and the formal 移动电话 in everyday usage. 玩手机 wán shǒujī = "to play on one's phone" (ubiquitous activity).
顺便说一下	shùn biàn shuō yī xià	"By the way — "By the way." Marks an aside or a piece of side information that occurs to you mid-conversation. 顺便 literally means "in passing." The English equivalent fits cleanly. Often shortened in casual speech to just 顺便.
顺便问一下	shùn biàn wèn yī xià	"While I'm at it, let me ask…" Lets you slide an unrelated question into the conversation without it feeling like an interruption. Pairs naturally with 顺便说一下 (the side-remark version).
说到这个	shuō dào zhè ge	"Speaking of which" or "since you mention it — "Speaking of which" or "since you mention it." Pivots to a related topic that the previous remark suggested. Cleaner than 对了 when the connection is more thematic than associative.
说得对	shuō de duì	"Well said" or "you're right — "Well said" or "you're right." Compliments the rightness of what was just said. Slightly more formal than 没错; acknowledges the other person's reasoning, not just the conclusion.
说实话	shuō shí huà	"To tell the truth" or "honestly — "To tell the truth" or "honestly." Signals that what follows is candid, sometimes uncomfortably so. Often used before a confession, an admission, or a frank criticism. The English "honestly" works as a direct translation.
所以	suǒ yǐ	therefore; so; that is why — 所 (that which) + 以 (the means/reason by which). The most common consequential conjunction in modern Chinese. Follows a cause clause (often introduced by 因为) and introduces the result: 因为下雨，所以我没去 (because it rained, so I did not go).
特别是	tè bié shì	"Especially" or "particularly — "Especially" or "particularly." Singles out the most important case within a broader claim. 我喜欢中国菜, 特别是川菜 ("I like Chinese food, especially Sichuan food"). Direct, useful, common.
停电	tíng diàn	power outage
完全同意	wán quán tóng yì	"Completely agree — "Completely agree." Strong endorsement. Used when you want to back the speaker without reservation. The 完全 ("completely") makes it emphatic; reserve for moments when full agreement is what you actually mean.
网络	wǎng luò	internet; network
委婉	wěi wǎn	tactful, indirect
我不太同意	wǒ bù tài tóng yì	"I don't quite agree — "I don't quite agree." The 不太 ("not too") softens a disagreement into something closer to "I'm not sure about that." Polite, leaves room for further discussion. Common in considered conversations where you want to push back without confrontation.
我的意思是	wǒ de yì si shì	"What I mean is — "What I mean is." Used to clarify when you sense you have been misunderstood, or to preface a more careful restatement of your point. Roughly equivalent to English "what I'm trying to say is…"
我个人认为	wǒ gèr én rèn wéi	"Personally, I think — "Personally, I think." The 个人 (gèrén, "personal") explicitly marks the view as your own and not a claim about consensus. Common in discussions and slightly more formal exchanges. Useful for stating a minority opinion without inviting argument.
我觉得	wǒ ju é de	"I feel" or "I think — "I feel" or "I think." The default opinion frame in spoken Chinese. Softer than 我认为 (wǒ rènwéi, "I hold the view that"), which is the more formal written-register equivalent. 我觉得 carries no special weight; it is the natural way to introduce any personal take.
我明白了	wǒ míng bai le	I understand now; I get it (I have entered a state of understanding) — State-change verbs like 明白 (understand), 知道 (know), 懂 (grasp), 累 (tired), 饿 (hungry), 高兴 (happy) pair powerfully with sentence-final 了. The 了 marks the entry into that state , the moment the change occurs. Before: didn't understand. After: understands.
我也这么觉得	wǒ yě zhè me ju é de	"I feel the same way" or "I think so too — "I feel the same way" or "I think so too." Personal endorsement that aligns your view with the speaker's. Warmer than a simple agreement; signals shared perspective.
我有不同的看法	wǒ yǒu bù tóng de kàn fǎ	"I see it differently" or "I have a different view — "I see it differently" or "I have a different view." The most direct of the gentle disagreement frames. Acknowledges the other view exists and explicitly marks your own as separate. Useful when you actually want to make the disagreement clear.
五行	wǔ xíng	the five phases; the five agents — 五 wǔ + 行 xíng (to move; phase; agent). Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water, understood as five phases of cyclical change rather than fixed substances. The generating cycle (相生) runs Wood feeds Fire, Fire makes Earth (ash), Earth bears Metal, Metal carries Water, Water nourishes Wood.
喜庆	xǐ qìng	Auspicious, festive — Auspicious, festive: the quality of being celebratory and lucky. Red is described as 喜庆 by default; white and black are explicitly not 喜庆 in gift or festive contexts.
小时	xiǎo shí	hour (unit of time) — 小 xiǎo (small) + 时 shí (time; hour). A small slice of time: one hour. The compound reflects a traditional view of time's granularity , the hour as the smallest standard unit. Contrast 小时候 xiǎoshíhou (childhood, "when one was small"), where 小时 carries the older sense of "young age."
也就是说	yě jiù shì shuō	"In other words" or "what I mean is — "In other words" or "what I mean is." The restatement marker. Used to reframe something you just said in clearer or simpler terms. Indispensable when you sense the listener has not quite caught your point.
也许吧	yě xǔ ba	"Maybe so — "Maybe so." Noncommittal acknowledgment that signals you are not convinced but are not interested in arguing the point. The 吧 keeps it soft. Functionally close to English "I guess" or "could be."
一次性筷子	yī cì xìng kuài zi	Disposable chopsticks — Disposable chopsticks: the individually wrapped bamboo or wood pairs given at most restaurants. Billions are used annually; their environmental cost is a topic of ongoing public debate.
因此	yīn cǐ	"Therefore" or "for this reason — "Therefore" or "for this reason." More formal than 所以, common in written and considered spoken contexts. Often used to introduce a conclusion drawn from preceding points: 因此, 我们决定… ("therefore, we decided…").
由于	yóu yú	due to; owing to; because of — 由 yóu (from) + 于 yú (at; in). A formal, written near-synonym of 因为, marking the cause at the front of a sentence: 由于天气原因，航班取消了 "owing to the weather, the flight was cancelled." It pairs with 因此 rather than 所以.
有空再联系	yǒu kòng zài lián xì	"Keep in touch when you have time — "Keep in touch when you have time." Slightly more open-ended than 改天再聊. Suggests that contact will resume when both schedules permit. Polite and standard.
语气	yǔ qì	tone of voice; grammatical mood; modal register — 语 yǔ (language; speech) + 气 qì. The qi of language, how something is said rather than what. In grammatical analysis, 语气 = mood (indicative, imperative, interrogative).
原来如此	yuán lái rú cǐ	"Oh, so that's how it is!" or "I see now — "Oh, so that's how it is!" or "I see now." The classic "aha" of comprehension. The pieces have just clicked together. Slightly literary in feel but used constantly in everyday speech. The looser 原来是这样 (yuánlái shì zhèyàng) is the more colloquial version.
再说	zài shuō	we'll talk about it later; let's deal with it when the time comes; moreover (discourse marker) — 再 (later; further) + 说 (to speak; to deal with). Multiple uses: (1) Deferral: 这件事以后再说 (We'll deal with this later , let's not now). Very common for postponing a conversation or decision. (2) Discourse marker meaning "moreover; besides": 再说，我也没空 (Besides, I'm not free either).
在我看来	zài wǒ kàn lái	"In my view — "In my view." Slightly more formal than 我觉得, and more deliberately frames what follows as a personal perspective. Useful when you want to signal that you have actually considered the question, not just reacting.
怎么说呢	zěn me shuō ne	"How should I put it…" Buys a moment while signaling that you are searching for the right phrasing. Implies you have a thought but are weighing how to express it, which sounds thoughtful rather than blank. Useful before delivering a nuanced opinion.
这倒未必	zhè dào wèi bì	"That's not necessarily so — "That's not necessarily so." A more literary pushback, often used in discussions or debates. 倒 marks the contrary tone; 未必 means "not necessarily." Slightly considered, signals careful disagreement.
这个嘛	zhè ge ma	"Well, this…" or "as for this…" The 嘛 particle gives the phrase a casual, considering tone. Often used to introduce a response to a question that does not have a simple answer. Roughly: "well, about that…"
这就是为什么	zhè jiù shì wèi shén me	"That's why — "That's why." Used after explaining a cause to land the consequence with emphasis. 这就是为什么我不喜欢他 ("that's why I don't like him"). Slightly emphatic; signals you have just arrived at the punchline.
这么说吧	zhè me shuō ba	"Let me put it this way — "Let me put it this way." A colloquial reframing marker, used when you are about to take another run at explaining something. The 吧 particle softens it and invites the listener to follow you into the next attempt.
真的假的	zhēn de jiǎ de	"Seriously?" Literally "true or fake," used as an emphatic version of 真的吗 when something sounds surprising or hard to believe. Common among younger speakers and friends; slightly informal for use with elders or in professional settings.
真的吗	zhēn de ma	really? for real? — The most common follow-up question in casual conversation, expressing surprise. 真的 on its own answers it: "Yes, really." Often softened to 真假 (zhēn jiǎ, "real or fake?") in playful or informal speech.
钟	zhōng	clock; o'clock
重点是	zhòng diǎn shì	"The point is" or "the main thing is — "The point is" or "the main thing is." Close cousin to 关键在于, slightly more conversational. Often used to refocus a discussion that has drifted: 重点是, 我们要不要去 ("the point is, are we going or not").
总之	zǒng zhī	"In short" or "all in all — "In short" or "all in all." Conclusion marker that wraps up an explanation. Signals you have said your piece and are now landing the summary: 总之, 我觉得不错 ("all in all, I think it's fine").
最重要的是	zuì zhòng yào de shì	"Most importantly — "Most importantly." Marks the top-priority item in a list or argument. 最重要的是健康 ("the most important thing is health"). Useful for ranking points by significance.
